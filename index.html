My Story

From rock bottom to building a movement that saves lives

I grew up in the care system, never finding stability. Addiction, depression, C-PTSD, and years of struggle followed — including 30 years driving HGVs in waste management. Fitness became my way out. It gave me structure, discipline, and hope.

With the support of my wife, I qualified as a Level 3 Personal Trainer, gained CIMSPA status, and founded New Mindset New Power. What started as survival is now a thriving community helping people in recovery, women navigating menopause, older adults, and anyone ready to reset their life.

My mission: Build strong bodies, strong minds, and strong communities — one person at a time.
